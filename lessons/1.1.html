1.1 · What Claude Actually Is

Before you can master Claude, you need the right mental model. Most people carry a wrong one — and it costs them every single day.

In one breath: Claude is a prediction engine that read a huge slice of human writing and was then trained to be helpful, honest and harmless — it generates answers fresh every time instead of looking them up.

A prediction engine, not a database

Claude is a large language model (LLM) built by Anthropic , an AI safety company founded in 2021. Here is the plainest true description of how it works: During training, it read an enormous amount of human text — books, articles, code, conversations. From all that reading, it learned deep statistical patterns: which words, ideas and structures follow which. When you send it a message, it generates a response one small chunk at a time, each chunk chosen by asking «given everything so far, what comes next?» Think of a session musician who has played ten thousand songs. Ask for «something jazzy in a minor key» and she doesn't look up a song — she produces one, note by note, shaped by everything she has absorbed. That is Claude with language. It is why Claude can write a poem about your dog in the style of a pirate — no database contains that. It is also why Claude can occasionally state a wrong «fact» with total confidence: it is composing, not quoting. We deal with that properly in Lesson 1.6.

Trained to be helpful, honest, harmless

Raw pattern-prediction alone would make a strange assistant. So after the reading phase, Anthropic shapes the model's behaviour. Two ideas are worth knowing by name: Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF): humans compare pairs of model answers, and the model is nudged toward the kind people prefer — clearer, more useful, less evasive. Constitutional AI: Anthropic's signature method. The model is given a written set of principles — a constitution — and learns to critique and revise its own answers against those principles, rather than relying only on human raters. This is why Claude has a recognisable character: it explains its reasoning, admits uncertainty, pushes back on harmful requests, and generally behaves like a thoughtful colleague rather than a slot machine. Anthropic's stated mission — building AI that is safe and beneficial — is not marketing garnish; it shapes product decisions you will notice throughout this course.

What Claude is not

Clearing three misconceptions now will save you a hundred frustrations later: Not a search engine. Claude's core knowledge comes from training, which has a cutoff date. For anything recent it must use its web search tool (it can — Lesson 4.6). Untangling «what the model knows» from «what it can look up» is a professional skill. Not a person. No feelings to hurt, no ego to bruise. You can demand three rewrites, say «that was wrong, fix it», or abandon a thread mid-sentence. Politeness costs nothing, but hesitation costs plenty. Not a mind-reader. Claude sees exactly the words you send and nothing else. Not your screen, not your intentions, not the context in your head. The whole craft of prompting — Phase B of this course — is the skill of transferring what is in your head into words.

Recap

Claude generates — it does not look up. It sees only the words you send, so context and constraints are how you steer the prediction. Fluent confidence is not evidence of truth.
