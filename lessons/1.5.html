1.5 · Plans & Pricing: What's Worth Paying For

Free, Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise — and the API's pay-per-use meter. What each actually buys, and a decision rule that doesn't waste your money.

In one breath: Claude pricing is a ladder — Free to try, Pro (~$17–20/mo) for serious individual use with Cowork and Claude Code, Max ($100+/mo) for heavy daily leverage, Team/Enterprise for companies — plus the separate pay-per-token API; pay for the rung whose ceiling you actually hit.

The ladder, rung by rung (July 2026)

Plan Price What it really means Free $0 Real Claude, capped usage that resets in 5-hour windows. Web, desktop, mobile; files, web search, basic features. Perfect for learning — you can complete a large share of this course on it. Pro ~$17/mo annual (~$20 monthly) The serious-individual rung: at least 5× Free usage, unlimited Projects, Research mode, connectors, more model choice — and the agentic surfaces: Cowork and Claude Code . This is where most of this course's power unlocks. Max from $100/mo (5×) to a 20× tier For people whose workday runs on Claude: 5–20× Pro usage, higher output limits, priority access, earliest features. Buy when you're hitting Pro's ceiling and the lost time costs more than the upgrade. Team ~$20–25/seat (standard) · ~$100–125 (premium seats) Companies: central billing, admin controls, collaboration; premium seats carry the heavy Cowork/Code allowances. Enterprise custom / seat + usage SSO, audit logs, compliance controls, data-governance machinery. Two facts people miss: your subscription's usage is a token budget in disguise — heavy Cowork and Claude Code sessions consume it much faster than chat (they process far more tokens per task). And the API is a separate meter entirely (per-token, Lesson 1.3's prices) — subscriptions don't include API credits, and vice versa.

The decision rule

Work the ladder like an operator: Start Free, but push it hard. Not «try a few questions» — attempt real work until you personally feel the usage ceiling. Now you know what the next rung buys: your bottleneck, not a feature list. Upgrade to Pro when Claude touches money or career. The arithmetic is absurd at almost any wage: if Pro saves two hours a month, it's paid for itself several times over. And Cowork + Claude Code aren't luxuries — they're where the leverage lives (Phases C–D). Max is a business decision, not a fan purchase. Buy the 5× tier when you're regularly rate-limited on Pro and waiting costs you billable time. The 20× tier is for Claude-all-day operators — by Track 11 you may be one. The API is for builders and volume — you pay per token, no session caps, and it powers things you ship to others. Many operators run both: a subscription for their hands-on work, API for their products. Track 9 does the full cost math. The reframe that matters: stop asking «is $20 expensive?» and ask «what does one reclaimed workday per month earn me?» In India as much as in the US, the subscription question is really a wage-arbitrage question — and for freelancers in Track 11, plan costs become a line item their clients effectively pay for.

Recap

Pay for the rung whose ceiling you actually hit: Free to learn, Pro when Claude touches income, Max when waiting costs more than upgrading — and remember the API is a separate meter for things you build.
