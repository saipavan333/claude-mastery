13.4 · Job-Hunting With Claude

Job-hunting with Claude: using the very AI and skills you've mastered to find opportunities, tailor applications, and run a job search most candidates can't match — an unfair advantage you should use.

In one breath: Use Claude to run a superior job search — researching companies and roles, tailoring applications to each role, preparing for interviews, and managing the process — turning the very AI you've mastered into your job-hunt advantage, because a candidate who uses AI expertly to job-hunt out-executes one who doesn't, and it demonstrates the skills employers want.

Research: know the company and role deeply

Research companies with Research mode (4.6): before applying/interviewing, deeply research the company — what they do, their AI initiatives, recent news, their challenges, their culture. A candidate who deeply understands the company stands out enormously (most do surface research). Research mode does this thoroughly, fast. Decode the role: analyze the job description with Claude — what are they REALLY looking for (beyond the listed requirements), what problems is this role solving, what would make a candidate stand out? Understanding the role's true needs lets you position yourself precisely. Find opportunities: use Claude to help identify target companies (ones doing AI work that fits your skills), roles that match your portfolio (13.3), and where your specific combination (operator+builder, or AI-native [your field]) fits. Targeted searching beats spray-applying. Understand the market: research realistic comp (13.1's data + current listings), what the role typically requires, and how your profile compares — so you target well and negotiate informed.

Tailor: applications that fit each role precisely

Tailor every application — the generic résumé/application loses to the tailored one. Use Claude to tailor your résumé and cover letter to EACH role: emphasizing the portfolio projects (13.3) and skills most relevant to THAT role, using the company's language, addressing THAT role's real needs (from your research). Tailoring at scale is exactly what AI excels at — you can tailor deeply for every application where others send generic ones. Lead with proof: your applications should foreground your PORTFOLIO (13.3) — 'here's a working AI product I built that's relevant to this role' — because proof beats claims (13.3). Claude helps you frame your proof compellingly for each specific role. Show you understand THEIR needs: a tailored application demonstrates you understand the company and role (from research) and positions your proof against their specific needs — vastly more compelling than a generic 'I'm skilled at AI' application. This is the value-first principle (11.3) applied to job-hunting. The honest note: tailor and enhance with AI, but keep it TRUE and YOURS — AI helps you present your real ability and proof compellingly, not fabricate. Authentic, AI-enhanced applications; not fabricated ones (your honesty discipline, 1.6, applies to your own applications).

Prepare and manage: out-execute the process

Interview prep with Claude (13.5 details this): practice answers, anticipate questions, prepare your project stories (13.3), rehearse — Claude is your interview coach, available anytime. Preparation is a huge edge, and AI makes thorough prep easy. Prepare your questions: research-informed questions FOR the interviewer (about their AI work, the role's challenges) demonstrate genuine interest and understanding — Claude helps you prepare thoughtful questions that impress. Manage the process: use Claude to track applications, prepare for each stage, draft follow-ups, and manage the logistics of a job search (which can be overwhelming) — your job-search project manager. An organized, well-managed search beats a chaotic one. The meta-advantage: a candidate who uses AI expertly throughout their job search out-executes one who doesn't — deeper research, better-tailored applications, thorough prep, organized process. AND doing so DEMONSTRATES the AI skill employers want ('this candidate clearly knows how to use AI to be more effective — exactly what we need'). Your Claude mastery is both the qualification AND the job-hunt advantage. Balance with authenticity: use AI to out-execute the process, but the interview and relationship are human — AI prepares you superbly, but YOU show up authentically. AI-enhanced preparation, authentic human presence.

Recap

Weaponize your Claude mastery for the job search itself — deep company research (Research mode), precise per-role tailoring (proof-forward, true), thorough interview prep, and organized management — to out-execute candidates who don't, while DEMONSTRATING the exact AI skill employers want; your Claude skills are simultaneously the qualification, the job-hunt advantage, and the proof, so use all three (staying true and authentic).
